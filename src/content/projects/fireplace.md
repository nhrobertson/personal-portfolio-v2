FiReplace is made up of a main control unit, a remote control, and a temperature sensor, all communicating over ESP-NOW rather than normal WiFi or dedicated RF — chosen for simplicity. All hardware is being developed alongside the firmware and requirements.

This is an actively worked-on project — check back for updates.
